## RoleColorFinder — Co-Founder & CTO

RoleColorFinder is a leadership-style assessment. It takes users through a short set of questions grounded in team psychology to identify their dominant color style, then turns that into a practical read on how they lead and where they contribute best, along with a fit check against different role types. It's built for students, educators, and early-career professionals, with individual and team-based results for schools and career-readiness programs.

<img src="assets/rolecolor-styles.svg" alt="RoleColorFinder outcomes: Yellow, Red, Green, Blue" width="420">

As CTO, I lead engineering for the product end to end, from the backend and data layer to the frontend people take the assessment on. The stack centers on FastAPI and PostgreSQL on the backend with a Next.js frontend. The codebase is private, but the assessment itself is live:

**[rolecolorfinder.com →](https://www.rolecolorfinder.com/)**
